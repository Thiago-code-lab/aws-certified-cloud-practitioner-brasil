# Frequent Errors

Short list for corrective review. The idea is to reduce recurring errors, not to cover the entire certification.

## Fundamentals
1. Confusing high availability with high performance.
2. Treating Region and AZ as synonyms.
3. Forgetting that shared responsibility changes depending on the service, but data and permissions remain critical to the customer.

## Security
4. Assuming that AWS configures IAM permissions for the customer.
5. Using the root user for routine work.
6. Substituting CloudTrail for CloudWatch in audit questions.

## S3 and storage
7. Ignoring retrieval costs and retrieval time in archive classes.
8. Treating S3 as an instance disk.
9. Confusing versioning with lifecycle.

## Costs
10. Choosing the lowest price per hour without looking at the total cost.
11. Forgetting to configure alerts with Budgets.
12. Using Cost Explorer for prior estimation when the clue points to Pricing Calculator.

## Practical fix
For each block of questions, record 3 recurring errors and review only these points for 15 minutes on the same day.
