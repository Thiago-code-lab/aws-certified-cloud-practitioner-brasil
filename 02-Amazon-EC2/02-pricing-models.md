# EC2 Pricing Models

EC2 purchasing options balance flexibility, predictability, and discounts.

- **On-Demand:** no long-term commitment; suitable for new or unpredictable workloads.
- **Savings Plans:** discounts for committing to a consistent hourly spend for one or three years.
- **Reserved Instances:** billing discounts for a committed instance configuration over one or three years. Regional reservations do not reserve capacity; zonal reservations include capacity in a specific AZ.
- **On-Demand Capacity Reservations:** reserve capacity in a specific AZ; immediate-use reservations have no term commitment, while future-dated reservations can require one. These reservations do not provide a discount by themselves.
- **Spot Instances:** unused EC2 capacity at major discounts, but subject to interruption.
- **Dedicated Hosts:** dedicated physical servers for isolation or specific licensing needs.
- **Exam tip:** interruption-tolerant workloads suggest Spot; steady usage suggests a longer-term commitment.
