# AWS Billing Dashboard

The Billing and Cost Management console centralizes invoices, payments, taxes, preferences and cost tools.

- The Bills page details charges by service and account.
- Cost Explorer deepens the analysis; Budgets creates alerts.
- Billing permissions must follow least privilege.
- **Exam tip:** consulting the current invoice points to Billing; Investigating trends points to Cost Explorer.
