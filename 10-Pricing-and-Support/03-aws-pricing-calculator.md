# AWS Pricing Calculator

AWS Pricing Calculator estimates the monthly cost of an architecture before deployment.

- Allows you to combine services, Regions, volumes and purchase models.
- Useful for comparing alternatives and documenting assumptions.
- The estimate depends on the accuracy of the data provided and does not replace the actual invoice.
- **Exam tip:** prediction of a future workload points to Pricing Calculator; analysis of the expenditure that occurred points to Cost Explorer.
