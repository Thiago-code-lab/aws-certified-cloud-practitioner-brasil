# Compliance Programs

Compliance programs demonstrate that AWS meets recognized controls and standards, depending on the service and Region.

- AWS provides evidence about its share of shared responsibility.
- The customer still configures its load and processes according to the applicable requirements.
- Eligibility varies by service; confirm the scope before deploying.
- **Test tip:** using AWS does not automatically make an application compliant with a standard.
