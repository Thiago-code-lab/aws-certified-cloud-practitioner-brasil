Module 12

AWS Cloud Adoption Framework

AWS CAF organizes cloud adoption through six perspectives and four iterative transformation phases. These lessons provide an introductory overview.

The 4 Phases of the Journey

E
Envision
Identify and prioritize transformation opportunities with measurable impact on the business.
A
Align
Identify gaps between current and desired states in all 6 CAF perspectives.
L
Launch
Deliver pilot projects into production and demonstrate incremental value of investment.
S
Scale
Expand pilot projects to full production and achieve business benefits.

Module 13

Well-Architected Framework

The 6 pillars that guide the construction of secure, resilient, efficient and sustainable systems on AWS — all documented with design principles and services.

Pillar 01
🏆
Operational Excellence
Execute and monitor systems to deliver business value and continually improve.
  • Execute operations as code (IaC)
  • Small, reversible changes
  • Anticipate and learn from failures
Pillar 02
🔐
Security
Protect information, systems and assets with ongoing risk assessments and compliance.
  • Least privilege in identity
  • Security at every layer
  • Data in transit and at rest
Pillar 03
⚡
Reliability
Ensure workloads perform their intended function correctly and consistently.
  • Automatically recover from crashes
  • Test recovery procedures
  • Manage changes by automation
Pillar 04
🚀
Performance Efficiency
Use computing resources efficiently to meet requirements and maintain performance.
  • Democratize advanced technologies
  • Global scale in minutes
  • Serverless architectures
Pillar 05
💰
Cost Optimization
Avoid unnecessary expenses and maintain full control of where money is invested.
  • Cloud Financial Management
  • Elastic Consumption Model
  • Analyze and assign expenses
Pillar 06
🌱
Sustainability
Minimize environmental impacts of workloads with a focus on energy efficiency and CO₂ reduction.
  • Establish environmental impact goals
  • Maximize resource utilization
  • Use efficient managed services
