# Amazon SageMaker AI

Amazon SageMaker brings together managed resources to create, train, tune, and deploy machine learning models.

- Serves data preparation, experiments, training and inference.
- Reduces the need to administer ML infrastructure.
- It is recommended when the team needs to develop or operate its own models.
- **Exam tip:** complete ML lifecycle points to SageMaker; consuming a ready-made foundation model can point to Bedrock.

Deep dive into AWS architecture: https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-brasil/tree/saa-c03-english

## Current name

The managed model-building service described here is Amazon SageMaker AI. The broader Amazon SageMaker offering also includes data and analytics capabilities.
