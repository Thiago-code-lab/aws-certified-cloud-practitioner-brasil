<details>
<summary><strong>View answer</strong></summary>

## Card 07
**Question:** Does Free Tier mean "I won't pay anything on AWS"?

<details>
<summary><strong>View answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
No. Eligibility depends on account creation date, account plan, credits, and service limits. Eligible new accounts can receive credits with a six-month free account plan; paid-plan usage beyond eligible offers or credits can be billed. Check current terms before provisioning.


[AWS reference](https://aws.amazon.com/free/)

</details>

## Card 10
**Question:** Which current paid support plan provides production technical support beyond Basic?

<details>
<summary><strong>View answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Business Support+ is the current entry paid plan for production technical support. Developer and Business are legacy plans unavailable for new subscriptions and scheduled for discontinuation on January 1, 2027. Compare the current support offerings before choosing a plan.


[AWS reference](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/faqs/)

</details>
