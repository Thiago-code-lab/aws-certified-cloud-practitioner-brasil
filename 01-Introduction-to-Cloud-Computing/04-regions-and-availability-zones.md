# AWS Regions and Availability Zones

An AWS Region is a geographic area made up of multiple isolated Availability Zones (AZs). Deploying across AZs limits the impact of a single-site failure.

- **Region:** select according to latency, pricing, compliance, and service availability.
- **AZ:** use at least two for high availability within a Region.
- **Edge locations:** help bring services such as CloudFront closer to users.
- **Exam tip:** multiple AZs increase local availability; multiple Regions address broader geographic recovery.
